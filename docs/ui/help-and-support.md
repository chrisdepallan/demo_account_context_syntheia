# Help and Support in the Dashboard

## How do I open in-app chat?

Select the **question-mark icon at the bottom right** of the dashboard. Live chat is available on **Pro and above**, during business hours (Monday–Friday, 08:00–18:00 US Central Time).

## Where else can users get help?

- Help center: `https://help.acme-cloud.example` (all plans)
- Email: `support@acme-cloud.example` (all plans)
- Phone: Business and Enterprise
- 24/7 for P1 incidents and a named Customer Success Manager: Enterprise

## Where do I check if the service is down?

The status page: `https://status.acme-cloud.example`. P1 incidents are posted there within **15 minutes**.

## How do I escalate a ticket?

Reply "escalate" on the ticket. A team lead reviews it within **1 business day**. Business and Enterprise customers can also call the support line.
