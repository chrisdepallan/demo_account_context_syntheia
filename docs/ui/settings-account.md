# Settings → Account

Only the **Owner** sees the actions on this page.

## How do I transfer account ownership?

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Transfer ownership**.
2. Pick an existing **Admin** as the new Owner.
3. The new Owner must accept within **7 days**, or the request expires.

After the transfer, the previous Owner becomes an Admin. If the Owner has left and cannot be reached, an Admin must contact support (needs written confirmation from a company officer; up to **5 business days**).

## How do I close the account?

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Close account**.
2. Type the account name to confirm.
3. The subscription is cancelled, the account moves to `closed`, and all members are signed out.

Annual plans closed within **30 days** of purchase are refunded in full; otherwise there is no refund for unused time.

## Can I undo closing the account?

Yes, for **30 days**. The Owner uses the restore link in the closure email, or asks support. On day 31 the account becomes `deleted` and cannot be recovered by anyone.

## Why is the Close account button missing?

Only the Owner can close an account. Admins and other roles do not see the button.
