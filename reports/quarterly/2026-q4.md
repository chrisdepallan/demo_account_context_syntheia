## Which incidents happened in Q4 2026 so far?

| Incident | Date | Priority | Duration | What happened | Counted as downtime? |
|----------|------|----------|----------|---------------|----------------------|
| INC-2026-006 | 2026-10-15 | P3 | 5 days to detect | Revoked API keys worked for up to 15 minutes instead of 60 seconds in `ap-south` | No |

There was one incident, a P3. The key cache lifetime was set to 900 seconds in the `ap-south` config, so a revoked key could still be accepted for up to 15 minutes in that region. In `us-east` and `eu-central` revocation took effect within 60 seconds as documented.

- The problem is recorded with "5 days to detect" instead of a duration, because it was a wrong setting, not an outage.
- A related weakness had been reported by the external penetration test in September: the lifetime of the key cache came from per-region config and was not enforced in code.
- The fix shipped in release 2026.8 on 2026-11-10: the cache lifetime is fixed at 60 seconds in code. The runbook `docs/runbooks/leaked-api-key.md` was updated.
- In the affected period, 64 keys were revoked in `ap-south` workspaces. The Identity team checked the request logs for each one. For 3 keys, requests were accepted after revocation; all three were routine rotations by the customer, and none was a reported leak.
