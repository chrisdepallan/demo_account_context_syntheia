# Quarterly Service Report: Q2 2026

This report covers 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-30 (91 days). It summarises uptime, incidents, releases, support, billing and security for Acme Cloud. All data is fictional sample data. Customers are counted, not named.

## What were the headline numbers in Q2 2026?

| Measure | Q2 2026 | Q1 2026 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Uptime, `us-east` | 99.993% | 99.959% |
| Uptime, `eu-central` | 99.989% | 99.964% |
| Uptime, `ap-south` (from 2026-05-26) | 99.992% | Region did not exist |
| Incidents | 1 (P2) | 2 |
| Releases | 2 (2026.3 and 2026.4) | 2 |
| Support tickets | 4,420 | 4,180 |
| First responses within target | 96.6% | 94.8% |
| Failed-payment rate | 3.50% | 4.50% |
| Accounts that reached `read_only` | 122 | 148 |
| Refunds issued | 232, total $111,140 | 255, total $130,970 |
| Users with MFA | 66.8% | 61.4% |

Q2 was the quietest quarter of 2026. All three regions met the uptime target, there was no P1 incident, and support met its first-response goal on every plan.

## Did each region meet the 99.9% uptime target?

| Region | Period measured | Minutes in period | Minutes down | Uptime | Met 99.9%? |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|--------------|--------|------------|
| `us-east` | 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-30 | 131,040 | 9 | 99.993% | Yes |
| `eu-central` | 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-30 | 131,040 | 14 | 99.989% | Yes |
| `ap-south` | 2026-05-26 to 2026-06-30 | 51,840 | 4 | 99.992% | Yes |

Yes. All three regions were above 99.9%.

- `ap-south` (Mumbai) opened for Enterprise accounts on 2026-05-26 with release 2026.4. Its uptime is measured over 36 days, not the full quarter. The allowance for that period at 99.9% is 51.8 minutes, and 4 minutes were used.
- No incident counted as downtime this quarter. All minutes down were short interruptions of less than 5 minutes each: three in `us-east`, four in `eu-central` and one in `ap-south`.
- INC-2026-003 does not count as region downtime. It affected SAML logins for 38 workspaces. Password login, existing sessions and the API kept working.

## Which incidents happened in Q2 2026?

| Incident | Date | Priority | Duration | What happened | Counted as downtime? |
|----------|------|----------|----------|---------------|----------------------|
| INC-2026-003 | 2026-05-06 | P2 | 2 h 25 min | SAML logins were rejected with "signature invalid" for 38 workspaces | No |

There was one incident. Two auth nodes had a clock drift of 4 minutes, which is above the 3 minutes of clock skew allowed on SAML assertions. Logins that reached those two nodes were refused; logins that reached other nodes worked, which made the problem look random to customers.

Follow-up, shipped with release 2026.4 on 2026-05-26:

- An alert fires when the clock of any node drifts by 30 seconds.
- The runbook `docs/runbooks/sso-login-failures.md` tells the on-call engineer to check clock drift before asking a customer to upload a new certificate.

Support received 4,420 tickets, 240 more than in Q1 (up 5.7%). The growth was in P3 and P4. Urgent tickets fell: P1 tickets dropped from 38 to 21 because there was no P1 incident.

## Did support meet its first-response targets in Q2 2026?

| Plan | Tickets | Answered within target | Attainment | Q1 attainment |
|------|---------|------------------------|------------|---------------|
| Free | 640 | 630 | 98.4% | 98.0% |
| Starter | 930 | 905 | 97.3% | 96.7% |
| Pro | 1,510 | 1,450 | 96.0% | 94.0% |
| Business | 990 | 946 | 95.6% | 92.0% |
| Enterprise | 350 | 338 | 96.6% | 94.5% |
| **All plans** | **4,420** | **4,269** | **96.6%** | **94.8%** |

Yes. Every plan was at or above the team's goal of 95%. Business improved the most, from 92.0% to 95.6%.

What changed:

- Two more agents were moved to the phone and chat queue for Business and Enterprise from 2026-04-20.
- The business-hours calculation was fixed in April, so agents saw correct deadlines for tickets opened late on a Friday.
- All 21 P1 tickets were answered within target. For Enterprise that means within 30 minutes at any hour; the slowest Enterprise P1 response was 22 minutes.

## How many tickets were escalated, and what were they about?

- **84 escalations**, 1.9% of all tickets (Q1: 96).
- A team lead reviewed 82 of them within 1 business day (97.6%).

Top five ticket topics:

| Rank | Topic | Tickets |
|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Login, MFA and passkeys | 566 |
| 2 | SSO setup and SSO errors | 421 |
| 3 | Invoices and billing details | 402 |
| 4 | Plan changes and seats | 388 |
| 5 | UPI payments and the `ap-south` region | 173 |

Login and MFA questions were the top topic, with 566 tickets. About 190 of them were "how do I add a passkey?" in the four weeks after release 2026.3. INC-2026-003 produced 97 of the 421 SSO tickets. The most common question about `ap-south` was whether an existing workspace can be moved there. It cannot: the region is fixed when the workspace is created, so the customer must export the data and create a new workspace.

## How did billing perform in Q2 2026?

| Measure | Q2 2026 | Q1 2026 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Charges attempted | 43,600 | 41,200 |
| Charges that failed on the first try | 1,526 | 1,854 |
| Failed-payment rate | 3.50% | 4.50% |

The failed-payment rate fell to 3.50%, a full percentage point lower than in Q1. There was no processor outage, and since 2026-04-14 an outage would no longer start dunning.

What happened to the 1,526 failed payments:

| Outcome | Payments | Share of failed payments |
|---------|----------|--------------------------|
| Paid by the customer before the next retry | 259 | 17.0% |
| Recovered by the day 1 retry | 580 | 38.0% |
| Recovered by the day 3 retry | 290 | 19.0% |
| Recovered by the day 7 retry | 183 | 12.0% |
| Recovered by the day 14 retry | 92 | 6.0% |
| Not recovered: account became `read_only` on day 14 | 122 | 8.0% |
| **Total** | **1,526** | **100%** |

The four retries recovered 1,145 payments (75.0%). Customers paid another 259 themselves (17.0%), up from 14.0% in Q1; the `payment_failed` email now goes to all billing contacts, which seems to help. In total 122 accounts reached `read_only`, 26 fewer than in Q1.

UPI, new on 2026-05-26, was used for 640 charges by Indian accounts up to the end of June. Its failure rate was 2.8% (18 failures), below the overall rate.

Refunds issued, by approval tier:

| Refund amount | Approver | Refunds | Total amount |
|---------------|----------|---------|--------------|
| Up to $500 | Any support agent | 198 | $35,640 |
| $500.01 to $5,000 | Support team lead | 31 | $52,700 |
| Over $5,000 | Billing manager | 3 | $22,800 |
| **Total** | | **232** | **$111,140** |

Support issued 232 refunds worth $111,140, down from 255 refunds and $130,970 in Q1. Of the 232, 14 were goodwill refunds. The first-half access review checked a sample of these approvals (`reports/security/2026-h1-access-review.md`).

## What were the security numbers in Q2 2026?

| Measure | End of Q2 2026 | End of Q1 2026 |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| Active users | 191,300 | 182,400 |
| Users with at least one MFA method | 127,788 (66.8%) | 111,994 (61.4%) |
| Share of MFA users with a passkey | 4.1% | Not available before 2026.3 |
| Workspaces with **Enforce SSO** on | 247 | 212 |
| API keys revoked in the quarter | 1,265 | 1,140 |

MFA adoption rose by 5.4 percentage points to 66.8%, the largest quarterly rise of the year. Two things drove it: passkeys made setup easier, and Admins and Owners on Business and Enterprise who had used SMS were asked to choose another method. Workspaces with **Enforce SSO** grew from 212 to 247.
