# Quarterly Service Report: Q1 2026

This report covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-03-31 (90 days). It summarises uptime, incidents, releases, support, billing and security for Acme Cloud. All data is fictional sample data. Customers are counted, not named.

| Measure | Q1 2026 |
|---------|---------|
| Uptime, `us-east` | 99.959% (target 99.9%) |
| Uptime, `eu-central` | 99.964% (target 99.9%) |
| Incidents | 2 (one P1, one P2) |
| Releases | 2 (2026.1 and 2026.2) |

Both regions met the 99.9% uptime target. Support answered 94.8% of tickets within the first-response target, just under the team's goal of 95%. The failed-payment rate of 4.50% was pushed up by the payment processor outage on 2026-03-19.

## Did each region meet the 99.9% uptime target?

| Region | Minutes in quarter | Minutes down | Uptime | Target | Met? |
|--------|--------------------|--------------|--------|--------|------|
| `us-east` (Virginia) | 129,600 | 53 | 99.959% | 99.9% | Yes |
| `eu-central` (Frankfurt) | 129,600 | 47 | 99.964% | 99.9% | Yes |

Yes. Both regions met the target. A 99.9% target allows 129.6 minutes of downtime in a 90-day quarter. `us-east` used 53 minutes (41% of the allowance) and `eu-central` used 47 minutes (36%).

How uptime is measured:

- Probes sign in to the dashboard, call the API and make a test payment once per minute in each region.
- A minute counts as down when a core function fails for most users in the region.
- INC-2026-002 counts as 47 minutes of downtime in both regions. The dashboard stayed up, but card payments failed everywhere, and payment is a core function.
- The other 6 minutes in `us-east` were two short interruptions of 3 minutes each, on 2026-01-27 and 2026-02-24. Each was too short to open an incident.
- The `ap-south` region did not exist in Q1. It opened on 2026-05-26 with release 2026.4.

## Which incidents happened in Q1 2026?

| Incident | Date | Priority | Duration | What happened | Counted as downtime? |
|----------|------|----------|----------|---------------|----------------------|
| INC-2026-001 | 2026-02-11 | P2 | 3 h 10 min | Invoice emails were sent twice to billing contacts | No |
| INC-2026-002 | 2026-03-19 | P1 | 47 min | Card payments failed in all regions | Yes, 47 minutes in each region |

There were two incidents: one P2 and one P1.

- **INC-2026-001.** The retry in the notification queue was not idempotent, so a retried send produced a second email. No customer was charged twice; only the email was duplicated. Since release 2026.2, every send carries an idempotency key made of the template, the recipient and the object ID.
- **INC-2026-002.** The payment processor had an outage. There was no fallback, and dunning counted the failures, so healthy accounts moved to `past_due`. The status page at `https://status.acme-cloud.example` was updated 9 minutes after the start, inside the 15-minute rule for P1 incidents. The fix shipped in release 2026.3 on 2026-04-14: outages are detected and no longer count as dunning failures.

## Did support meet its first-response targets in Q1 2026?

The targets depend on plan and priority. For example, a P1 must get a first response within 30 minutes at any hour on Enterprise, within 1 business hour on Business and within 4 business hours on Pro. Free tickets have 3 business days at every priority.

| Plan | Tickets | Answered within target | Attainment |
|------|---------|------------------------|------------|
| Free | 610 | 598 | 98.0% |
| Starter | 890 | 861 | 96.7% |
| Pro | 1,420 | 1,335 | 94.0% |
| Business | 930 | 856 | 92.0% |
| Enterprise | 330 | 312 | 94.5% |
| **All plans** | **4,180** | **3,962** | **94.8%** |

Not fully. The support team's goal is to answer at least 95% of tickets within target on every plan. Free and Starter met it. Pro (94.0%), Business (92.0%) and Enterprise (94.5%) missed it.

Reasons for the misses:

- On 2026-03-19, 420 tickets arrived in one day, about nine times a normal day. Business customers have the shortest targets during business hours (1 business hour for P1, 4 business hours for P2), so they were hit hardest.
- Of the 38 P1 tickets, 33 were answered within target. All 5 late answers were on 2026-03-19.
- The numbers were recalculated after a bug in the business-hours calculation was fixed in April. Deadlines that crossed a Friday evening had been set on Saturday. With the corrected deadlines, 37 tickets that looked late were on time. The table above uses the corrected numbers.

## How did billing perform in Q1 2026?

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Charges attempted | 41,200 |
| Charges that failed on the first try | 1,854 |
| Failed-payment rate | 4.50% |
| Failures caused by INC-2026-002 | 312 |
| Failed-payment rate without those 312 | 3.74% |

The failed-payment rate was 4.50%. Without the 312 failures caused by the processor outage it would have been 3.74% (1,542 of 41,200).

What happened to the 1,854 failed payments:

| Outcome | Payments | Share of failed payments |
|---------|----------|--------------------------|
| Paid by the customer before the next retry (for example after a card update) | 260 | 14.0% |
| Recovered by the day 1 retry | 742 | 40.0% |
| Recovered by the day 3 retry | 371 | 20.0% |
| Recovered by the day 7 retry | 222 | 12.0% |
| Recovered by the day 14 retry | 111 | 6.0% |
| Not recovered: account became `read_only` on day 14 | 148 | 8.0% |
| **Total** | **1,854** | **100%** |

The four automatic retries recovered 1,446 payments, 78.0% of all failures. The day 1 retry alone recovered 40.0%. It was unusually strong this quarter because 294 of the 312 outage failures succeeded on day 1. In total 148 accounts reached `read_only`.

Refunds issued, by approval tier:

|---------------|----------|---------|--------------|

| $500.01 to $5,000 | Support team lead | 37 | $61,050 |

Support issued 255 refunds worth $130,970. Most refunds were small (214 of 255 were $500 or less), but the 41 larger ones made up 71% of the money. Of the 255 refunds, 19 were goodwill refunds, each approved by a team lead.

## What are the goals for Q2 2026?

1. **Support:** at least 95% first-response attainment on every plan, including Business.
2. **Billing:** a failed-payment rate under 4.0%, and release 2026.3 live so that a processor outage does not start dunning.
3. **Security:** MFA adoption of 65% or more, helped by passkeys in release 2026.3.
4. **Uptime:** 99.9% in every region, including `ap-south` from its opening on 2026-05-26.
5. **Process:** a runbook for payment processor outages (`docs/runbooks/payment-processor-outage.md`).
