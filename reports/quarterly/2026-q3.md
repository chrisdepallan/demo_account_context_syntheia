# Quarterly Service Report: Q3 2026

This report covers 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30 (92 days). It summarises uptime, incidents, releases, support, billing and security for Acme Cloud. All data is fictional sample data. Customers are counted, not named.

Q3 was the busiest quarter of 2026: three releases, two incidents and the highest ticket volume of the year. All regions still met the uptime target, but `eu-central` used more than half of its allowance in one incident. Billing and security numbers improved.

## Did each region meet the 99.9% uptime target?

| Region | Minutes in quarter | Minutes down | Uptime | Met 99.9%? | Share of allowance used |
|--------|--------------------|--------------|--------|------------|-------------------------|
| `us-east` | 132,480 | 11 | 99.992% | Yes | 8% |
| `eu-central` | 132,480 | 77 | 99.942% | Yes | 58% |
| `ap-south` | 132,480 | 0 | 100.000% | Yes | 0% |

Yes, but `eu-central` came closest to the limit of any region in 2026. A 99.9% target allows 132.5 minutes of downtime in a 92-day quarter. `eu-central` was down for 77 minutes: 72 minutes during INC-2026-005 on 2026-09-08 and one further interruption of 5 minutes on 2026-07-30.

- `us-east` had three short interruptions, 11 minutes in total.
- `ap-south` had no downtime in its first full quarter.
- INC-2026-004 (stuck exports) does not count as downtime. Exports were unavailable, but the dashboard, logins, payments and the API worked in every region.

## Which incidents happened in Q3 2026?

| Incident | Date | Priority | Duration | What happened | Counted as downtime? |
|----------|------|----------|----------|---------------|----------------------|
| INC-2026-004 | 2026-07-22 | P2 | 9 h 40 min | Workspace exports were stuck in `running` | No |
| INC-2026-005 | 2026-09-08 | P1 | 1 h 12 min | The dashboard was unavailable in `eu-central` | Yes, 72 minutes in `eu-central` |

There were two incidents, both linked to infrastructure behaviour that had not been tested.

- **INC-2026-004.** Release 2026.5 rewrote the export as a queued job. Fifteen days later an export worker lost its job lease, and nothing timed the job out. Because only one export can run per workspace, affected customers could not start a new one. Since release 2026.6, an export that runs longer than 24 hours is marked `failed`, and the runbook `docs/runbooks/stuck-export.md` exists. The gap had been named in the freeze run and in the export benchmark of 2026-07-13 to 2026-07-15, but the fix was scheduled for 2026.6 and the incident came first.
- **INC-2026-005.** The primary database in `eu-central` failed, and the failover did not promote the replica automatically. The on-call engineer promoted it by hand. The status page was updated 12 minutes after the start, inside the 15-minute rule. Since release 2026.7, automatic promotion is on in every region and is tested every quarter (`docs/runbooks/region-failover.md`).

## Did support meet its first-response targets in Q3 2026?

| Plan | Tickets | Answered within target | Attainment | Q2 attainment |
|------|---------|------------------------|------------|---------------|
| Free | 690 | 676 | 98.0% | 98.4% |
| Starter | 1,010 | 975 | 96.5% | 97.3% |
| Pro | 1,650 | 1,560 | 94.5% | 96.0% |
| Business | 1,110 | 1,032 | 93.0% | 95.6% |
| Enterprise | 405 | 381 | 94.1% | 96.6% |
| **All plans** | **4,865** | **4,624** | **95.0%** | **96.6%** |

Only in part. Overall attainment was 95.0%, exactly at the team's goal. But the goal applies to every plan, and three plans missed it: Pro (94.5%), Business (93.0%) and Enterprise (94.1%).

Reasons:

- INC-2026-004 lasted 9 hours 40 minutes and crossed the end of the business day. P2 tickets from Business customers (target: 4 business hours) queued up overnight in US Central Time.
- INC-2026-005 started in the European morning, outside US business hours. Enterprise P1 tickets are covered 24/7 with a 30-minute target; 6 of 17 Enterprise P1 tickets that day were answered late because only two agents were on the night shift.
- Webhooks went generally available on 2026-09-29, and setup questions arrived on Pro and Business in the last two days of the quarter.

- **118 escalations**, 2.4% of all tickets (Q2: 84).
- A team lead reviewed 109 of them within 1 business day (92.4%). This is the lowest value of the year.

Top five ticket topics:

| Rank | Topic | Tickets |
|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Workspace export | 688 |
| 2 | Login, password reset and MFA | 497 |
| 3 | Webhook setup and signatures | 341 |
| 4 | Dashboard availability in `eu-central` | 263 |

Workspace export was the top topic, with 688 tickets. Of these, 214 were opened during or just after INC-2026-004, and most of the rest asked about the new behaviour: the export is queued, only one can run per workspace, and the download link is valid for 7 days. Webhook tickets (341) came mostly from the preview period before general availability.

The four retries recovered 1,191 payments (76.0%). The share that ended in `read_only` fell to 7.5%, under the goal of 8%. The pattern across the retry days has been stable all year: about 39% on day 1, 19% on day 3, 12% on day 7 and 6% on day 14.
