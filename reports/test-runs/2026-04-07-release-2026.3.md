## Which automated test failed, and why?

### Failure 1: a first-response deadline fell on a Saturday

- Test: `tests/unit/support/test_sla.py::test_due_time_started_friday_afternoon_rolls_to_monday`
- What the test does: a Business customer opens a P2 ticket on Friday 2026-04-03 at 16:00 US Central Time. The Business P2 target is 4 business hours. Business hours are 08:00–18:00 US Central Time, Monday to Friday. Two hours fit on Friday (16:00 to 18:00). The remaining two hours fall on Monday. The expected deadline is Monday 2026-04-06 at 10:00.
- What happened: the function returned Saturday 2026-04-04 at 10:00.
- Cause: the business-hours calculation skipped the weekend only when the ticket was opened on a weekend. When the remaining time rolled over the end of a Friday, it moved to "the next day at 08:00" without checking the weekday.
- How it was found: the test was new. The Support Tools team wrote it after an agent noticed a deadline on a Saturday in the admin console.
- Fix: the roll-over now moves to the next business day. Three more tests were added: a P1 on Pro (4 business hours) opened on Friday at 17:00, a P3 on Business (1 business day, which is 10 business hours) opened on Friday at 09:00, and a P4 (3 business days) opened on a Thursday.
- Customer impact before the fix: none for the customer. The deadline shown to agents was too early, so tickets looked late on Monday morning when they were not. First-response numbers for Q1 2026 were recalculated with the corrected function before the quarterly report was published.

The fix was merged on 2026-04-08. The repeat run passed with 367 passed, 0 failed and 4 skipped.

## How was the INC-2026-002 follow-up tested?

INC-2026-002 on 2026-03-19 was a payment processor outage. Card payments failed in all regions for 47 minutes, and dunning counted those failures. The follow-up in this release has 8 new automated tests and 1 manual case.

- Automated: when the processor reports an outage, a failed charge does not move the account to `past_due` and does not use one of the four retries (days 1, 3, 7 and 14).
- Automated: when the outage ends, the charge is tried again and a real decline starts dunning at day 0.
- Manual `TC-BILL-025`: during a simulated outage, **Settings → Billing** shows a notice and the account stays `active`.

All 8 automated tests and the manual case passed.
